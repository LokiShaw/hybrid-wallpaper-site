Privacy Policy

Hybrid Wallpaper (com.hybrid.livewallpaper) · Effective 15 October 2026

Hybrid Wallpaper is a personal app. Everything you add to it stays on your phone, or in your own Google Drive if you turn on Drive backup. We run no servers and receive none of your data.

Information the app handles

  • Photos and videos you choose. Read from the files or folders you allow, to show them as your wallpaper.
  • Notes, pictures and voice recordings. Stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • PIN and fingerprint. The PIN is stored as a salted one-way hash. Fingerprint checks are done by Android.
  • Settings and playlists. Stored on your phone.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for the drive.file permission only. With it the app can see and change only the files it created itself, in a folder called "Hybrid Wallpaper backup" in your own Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

  • Every file is encrypted on your phone with a key sealed by your PIN before it is uploaded. Google and anyone with access to the Drive account see only encrypted data.
  • The app reads your Drive's email address and storage quota only to show them in the app's settings.
  • The sign-in is kept on your phone, inside the app's PIN-sealed backup. It is not sent to us.
  • You can stop Drive backup in the app at any time, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. Files already in Drive stay there until you delete them.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to store and restore your own backup, is never transferred to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never read by people.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not use the advertising ID.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, clear its storage or uninstall it. Delete the "Hybrid Wallpaper backup" folder in Google Drive to remove the cloud copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
